Start with what bothers you

Most people come in with one thing in mind: weak sound, an old screen, dim headlights or worn seats. Tell us that first. We plan the rest around it so you do not pay twice for wiring or panel removal.

What we modify

Car audio with speakers, amplifiers, bass tubes and damping. Android screens that look factory fitted. LED headlights and fog lamps. Seat covers, 7D mats and ambient lighting. PPF, ceramic coating and sun film.

Modifications that stay legal and safe

We tell you if a change could cause trouble with rules, insurance or the car warranty before we fit it. Lighting, horns and window film especially have limits. Check current applicable rules for your car.
