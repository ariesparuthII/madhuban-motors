Choose for your cleaning routine

Rubber, coir and layered 7D mats have different surfaces and cleaning needs. Think about wet shoes, dust, children and how often you remove the mats. Ask to handle the material before deciding.

Check the fit in the car

Ask how the proposed mats sit around the pedals, seat rails, footrest and rear floor. The mat needs to lie securely. At handover, check pedal movement and seat adjustment with the fitted set in place.

Compare coverage rather than labels

Confirm which seating rows are included, whether a removable top layer is supplied and how the edges sit. Starting prices are ₹1,800 with fitting; material and vehicle coverage affect the final quote.
