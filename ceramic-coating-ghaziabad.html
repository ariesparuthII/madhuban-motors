Preparation is part of the decision

Tell us about existing scratches, repainting and earlier treatments. Ask what cleaning and surface preparation are included in the proposed work. Do not compare two coating quotes only by the number of advertised layers.

Separate coating from film

Coating and PPF are different treatments. If you are comparing them, say what concerns you: washing, appearance, road debris or maintenance. Ask what the chosen product is intended to do and what it does not cover.

Plan the maintenance

Ask for the exact product, curing instructions, washing routine and written warranty conditions where applicable. A coated car still needs care. The quote depends on the car, condition, preparation and chosen product.

What to send for a useful quote

Your car brand, model and year, the upgrade you want, and a photo of the existing setup if relevant. We confirm the equipment, fitting and final price before work begins. Please call before travelling for a particular product.
