Questions people actually ask

Straight answers.

Why do I need a relay for a horn?

A powerful horn pulls several times the current of the factory unit. Run that through the original horn switch and the switch or the wiring will eventually fail. A relay lets the horn draw directly from the battery while the switch only sends a small signal. It costs very little and prevents an expensive problem.

Are loud aftermarket horns allowed?

Ordinary replacement horns are fine. Multi tone pressure horns and anything that mimics an emergency vehicle are not permitted and attract fines. We will fit you something effective and legal, and we will say so if you ask for something that is not.

Where do you mount it?

Behind the grille, positioned so it projects forward and does not sit where it collects water. Mounting position affects how loud it actually sounds from outside.

How long does it take?

Usually under an hour.

Is fitting charged separately?

No. Fitting and the relay wiring are included.
