Questions people actually ask

Straight answers.

Why does wiring matter for a light?

Because a lamp draws current the original circuit was never designed for. Tapped straight into the loom it heats the wiring and can melt it. We use a separate fused harness with a relay so the load sits on its own circuit. That is the whole difference between a job that lasts and one that causes trouble.

Will brighter headlamps blind other drivers?

They can, if fitted carelessly. Beam pattern matters more than brightness. We aim them properly and will tell you if a particular upgrade is a bad idea for your car.

Do LED bulbs last longer?

Yes, considerably, and they draw less current than the bulb they replace. We sell them as matched pairs so both sides look the same.

Can you do ambient lighting inside the cabin?

Yes, footwell, door and dashboard lighting, wired properly and switched with the ignition.

Is fitting extra?

No. Fitting is included. If a job needs work beyond the normal we tell you before we start.
