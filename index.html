OUR STORY

Three generations on GT Road.

First a restaurant, then a car shop. Same spot on GT Road, same Paruthi family.

  1. Before 1989
    Where it started

    Madhuban Restaurant

    Jagdish Chandra Paruthi opened Madhuban Restaurant on this spot near the GT Road tehsil. Lawyers from the kachehri came here to eat every day, and Madhuban became a name the whole road knew.

  2. 1989
    Madhuban Motors opens

    From lunch to car work

    In 1989 Jagdish Chandra Paruthi and his son Vivek Paruthi turned the restaurant into Madhuban Motors. Through the 90s the same lawyers and families who knew the restaurant started coming here for work on their cars.

  3. Today
    The legacy continues

    Vivek Paruthi, Bitoo Bhaiya

    Vivek Paruthi still runs the counter every day. Across the GT Road car market he is simply Bitoo Bhaiya, the name people ask for when they want the job done right.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to book an appointment?

No appointment needed for most accessories. Just walk in during shop hours. For larger jobs like full audio system or seat covers, WhatsApp us first so we can keep time aside for you.

How long does installation take?

Dashcam or reverse camera: 1–2 hours. Seat covers or mats: 2–3 hours. Android screen: 3–4 hours. Full audio system: half a day. We'll give you a time estimate when you arrive.

Do you work on all car brands?

Yes - Maruti, Hyundai, Tata, Mahindra, Honda, Toyota, Kia, MG, and more. We stock car-specific fitments so accessories fit properly, not just generically.

Are your products genuine?

Yes. We are authorised dealers for Pioneer, Sony, JBL, Alpine, 3M, Kenwood, and others. We never sell grey-market or duplicate products - that's a promise we've kept for 37 years.

Do you offer warranty on installation?

Yes. All our installation work carries a warranty. Product warranty is as per the brand. If anything is not right, bring the car back - we will sort it out.

Can I see prices before visiting?

WhatsApp us your car model and what you need. We will reply with clear pricing so there are no surprises when you arrive. No pressure, no upselling.
