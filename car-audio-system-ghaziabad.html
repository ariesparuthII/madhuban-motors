Questions people actually ask

Straight answers.

What should I upgrade first?

Speakers and door damping. Most people buy a bigger amplifier while the doors are still rattling. Seal the doors and an ordinary speaker sounds clean. That is what we tell people at the counter and it is what we will tell you.

What is damping and do I need it?

Damping sheets seal the metal inside the door so it stops resonating. It is sold per sheet, from Rs 599. Front doors take two sheets, four for double damping. Without it you are paying for power you cannot hear.

Active or passive bass tube?

An active tube has its own amplifier inside, so it is simpler and starts at Rs 3,999. A passive tube has no amplifier and needs one, but gives more control. We will recommend based on your car and budget.

Do you charge for fitting?

No. Fitting and labour are included in our prices. If a job needs work beyond the normal we tell you the cost before starting, never after.

How long will it take?

Almost always the same day. A full build with damping, amplifier and bass can run longer, and we will tell you before we start.
