Questions people actually ask

Straight answers.

Is fitting included in the screen price?

Yes. Fitting, wiring and the mounting fascia are all included. We do not charge extra labour. If your car needs something beyond the normal, a canbus adapter for example, we tell you the cost before we start.

Will it work with my steering controls?

On most cars yes, using a steering control interface. It depends on the exact car and screen combination, so message us your model and we will confirm before you buy.

Can I keep my factory stereo and still add a camera?

Yes, in many cars. Send us your model on WhatsApp and we will tell you what is possible without replacing the screen.

How long does fitting take?

Almost always the same day. Larger jobs that combine a screen with speakers, damping or seat covers can run longer, and we tell you before we begin.

Which brands do you fit?

We are authorised dealers for Sony, Blaupunkt, Pioneer, JBL, Alpine, Hertz, JVC and Lumax.
