Output a finished photoreal image, not a UI mockup.

Superb snug fitting over realistic seat forms, not loose covers, no plastic gloss, no inflated gaming-seat shapes. Warm natural workshop daylight, dark neutral background, shallow depth of field but upholstery details sharp. Right-hand-drive steering wheel just visible on far right, infotainment blank black and unbranded. Composition landscape 3:2, no people, no words, no logos, no badges or fake brand marks. This is an explicitly labelled AI material/fitting concept, not proof of actual leather composition or real customer work. Focus on texture, stitches, fit, and a believable car interior.
