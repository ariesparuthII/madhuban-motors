Choose the coverage first

A front-area package and a full-body application involve different areas and work. List the panels you want covered, including any edges or high-contact areas. Ask for the scope in writing so quotes can be compared fairly.

Ask about the paint and preparation

Tell us about repainting, existing film, coatings or visible damage. The surface condition affects what preparation should be discussed. Film is not a repair for dents or damaged paint; decide how existing defects will be handled before the application.

Know the film and aftercare

Ask for the exact film name, product details and warranty conditions. Review edges and finished panels at handover. Confirm the product-specific aftercare and what to do if an edge needs attention. We do not publish an invented per-car price.

What to send for a useful quote

Your car brand, model and year, the upgrade you want, and a photo of the existing setup if relevant. We confirm the equipment, fitting and final price before work begins. Please call before travelling for a particular product.

Is PPF the same as ceramic coating?

No. Film is a separate applied layer; a ceramic coating is a different surface treatment. Compare the intended use and maintenance before choosing.
