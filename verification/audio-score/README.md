Original sci-fi ambient sound preview, local only.

A small native Web Audio graph synthesizes an original sustained pad with detuned low voices, suspended upper harmony, slow swells and a quiet delay. No copyrighted recordings or soundtrack melodies, no sample libraries and no music download. The audio module and control styles are about 4 KB combined. No AudioContext or synthesis graph is created until the explicit Sound button tap. Fade in/out, mute/suspend, tab visibility pause and a readable 44px control are included. No preferences or personal data are stored.

Chrome checks at 390×844 and 1280×800 confirmed silence on arrival, starts on button tap, mute and suspended context, no media requests or page exceptions, control within viewport. The offline 22-second WAV is a local listening sample, ignored by Git and not a website dependency. Its peak is about 0.10 and RMS about 0.023 (no clipping); these do not establish perceived loudness on every device. iPhone Safari audio/hardware playback should be reviewed before publishing.

Audible autoplay needs user activation in typical browsers: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Media/Guides/Autoplay
