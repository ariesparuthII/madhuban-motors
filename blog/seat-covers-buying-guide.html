Seat Covers Guide

Leatherette vs fabric vs custom-fit: what actually matters.

Seat covers are the fastest way to change how a car feels inside, but the options are confusing if you have not bought them before.

Leatherette is the most popular choice for Delhi NCR weather. It wipes clean, holds up to daily use, and looks premium without the maintenance a real leather interior needs. Fabric breathes better in peak summer but shows stains more easily and needs more care.

"Custom-fit" is not a marketing word

It is the actual difference. A generic cover bunches up around the headrest and seatbelt cutouts within weeks. A cover cut for your exact car model and seat shape sits flat, does not shift when you get in and out, and actually protects the factory seat underneath instead of hiding wear and tear.

Colour and stitching

Darker colours hide dust between cleanings, lighter colours show the cabin off better in photos but need more upkeep. Contrast piping (like black and red, or cream and blue) is popular right now because it looks intentional rather than like an aftermarket add-on.

What it costs

Pricing starts at Rs 2,500 and depends on your car model and the finish you pick. Fitting takes 2-3 hours, so plan to leave the car for a few hours or come early in the day.
