Questions people actually ask

Straight answers.

Do you make covers for my model?

We cover all common models and make custom seats for Innova and larger vehicles. Send us your car model on WhatsApp and we will confirm the same day.

My car already came with seat covers. Should I change them?

Often not. Many newer and higher variant cars already come with covers fitted, and we will tell you honestly if yours does not need changing. That advice is free.

Which brands do you use?

Elegant and Autoform among others, plus the major seat cover companies. We will show you the material samples at the counter.

Is fitting extra?

No. Fitting is included in the price. We never add labour at the end.

Can I get towel seat covers?

Yes. These are made to order, usually for Innova and larger vehicles. Message us with your model.
