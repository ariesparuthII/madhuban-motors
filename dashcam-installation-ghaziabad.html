Questions people actually ask

Straight answers.

Why not just buy one online and plug it in?

You can, and plenty of people do. The problem is the cable. A dashcam hanging off the lighter socket dangles across the dash, comes loose, and stops recording without you noticing. We hardwire it to the fuse box so it starts with the car and the cable never shows.

Will it drain my battery?

Not the way we fit it. We wire it to a circuit that switches off with the ignition. If you want parking mode, that needs a different connection and we will explain the battery trade off honestly before you decide.

Can it record while the car is parked?

Some models can, with the right wiring. It draws power while parked, so it depends on your battery and how often you drive. We will tell you whether it makes sense for your car.

How long does fitting take?

Usually the same day, often within a couple of hours.

Do you charge for fitting?

No. Fitting and wiring are included in the price. Anything beyond the normal is quoted before we start.
